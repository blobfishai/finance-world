# SOP-AR-02 — Customer credit limits

> Accounts Receivable / Credit · effective 2026-01-01 · v3.4 · SIMULATION ONLY

Limits are set from the most recent external credit report and internal payment behaviour,
and are reviewed annually or on any adverse event. A limit increase above 250,000 requires
the Controller; above 1,000,000, the CFO.

Orders that would breach a limit are held for credit release, not cancelled. Release requires
either payment clearing the excess or a documented temporary uplift with an expiry date.

Limits are denominated in the customer's trading currency.
