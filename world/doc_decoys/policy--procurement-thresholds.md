# SOP-PR-03 — Purchasing thresholds

> Procurement · effective 2026-01-01 · v2.5 · SIMULATION ONLY

Spend below 5,000 may be committed on a corporate card or a low-value order. Between 5,000
and 50,000 requires a purchase order against a quotation. Above 50,000 requires three
quotations or a documented single-source justification.

Splitting a requirement to stay beneath a threshold is a control breach regardless of intent.

Purchase orders are raised before the commitment, never after the invoice.
