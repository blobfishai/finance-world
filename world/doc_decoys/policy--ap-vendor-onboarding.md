# SOP-AP-02 — Vendor onboarding

> Accounts Payable · effective 2026-01-01 · v4.0 · SIMULATION ONLY

A new vendor record is created only from a completed onboarding pack: legal entity name,
registered address, tax documentation, and banking details on the vendor's own letterhead.

Banking details are verified by callback to a number obtained independently of the request —
never a number supplied in the request itself. The verification is logged against the vendor
record with the caller, the number dialled and the person reached.

Onboarding does not authorise spend; a purchase order does.
