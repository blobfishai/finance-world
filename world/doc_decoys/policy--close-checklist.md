# SOP-GL-01 — Month-end close checklist

> Controllership · effective 2026-01-01 · v6.1 · SIMULATION ONLY

The close runs to a published calendar. Subledgers close before the general ledger; the
ledger closes before reporting. Each line has a named owner and a completion sign-off.

Standing items: bank reconciliations, subledger-to-GL tie-outs, accruals and prepayments,
intercompany agreement, payroll posting, revenue cut-off, and the balance-sheet review.

A line is complete when its reconciling items are explained, not merely when it is prepared.
