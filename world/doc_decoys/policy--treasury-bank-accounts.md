# SOP-TR-01 — Bank account administration

> Treasury · effective 2026-01-01 · v2.0 · SIMULATION ONLY

Opening or closing a bank account requires CFO approval and is recorded on the account
register. Signatory changes follow the same route and are reflected with the bank within five
business days.

Balances are swept to the primary operating account daily where the sweep agreement permits.
Accounts dormant for two consecutive quarters are reviewed for closure.
