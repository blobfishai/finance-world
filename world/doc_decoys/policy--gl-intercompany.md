# SOP-GL-08 — Intercompany balances

> Controllership · effective 2026-01-01 · v1.9 · SIMULATION ONLY

Intercompany balances are confirmed between entities each month before consolidation.
Differences are investigated by the entity that raised the later document. Balances agreed
between entities are eliminated on consolidation; unagreed differences are carried to the
close file with an owner and an expected resolution date.

Intercompany invoices are settled quarterly by net cash transfer.
