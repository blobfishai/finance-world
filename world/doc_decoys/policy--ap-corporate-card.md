# SOP-AP-05 — Corporate card administration

> Accounts Payable · effective 2026-01-01 · v2.2 · SIMULATION ONLY

Cards are issued to employees with a documented recurring spend need and are cancelled within
one business day of a leaver's last day. Monthly limits are set by grade and reviewed each
January by the cardholder's cost-centre owner.

Disputed card transactions are raised with the issuer within 60 days of statement date.
Personal spend on a corporate card is repaid by payroll deduction in the following cycle;
repeated occurrences withdraw the card.

Card spend is not a vendor obligation and never appears in a payment proposal.
