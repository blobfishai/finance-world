# SOP-FIN-01 — Financial records retention

> Finance Operations · effective 2026-01-01 · v1.4 · SIMULATION ONLY

Ledgers, statutory accounts and tax filings are retained for seven years. Supporting
documentation — invoices, receipts, contracts, bank statements — is retained for seven years
from the end of the period to which it relates.

Records under legal hold are retained until the hold is released, whatever the schedule says.
Destruction outside the schedule requires Finance Operations sign-off.
