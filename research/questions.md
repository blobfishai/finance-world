## Domain & value

| # | Question | Status | Answer (evidence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is the domain, precisely? | ✅ | A corporate finance team inside a large AI company (buyer: xAI finance team). Three workloads: (a) **ERP QA** — grounded AP/AR questions (balances, aged debt, open invoices, payment terms, cash discounts, collection letters) answered via MCP tools against a live ERP; (b) **public-entity research** — GAAP figures/ratios for real public companies with context; (c) **business briefs** — structured company profiles mixing public + internal ERP data. (User brief; task examples reference Dynamics demo world: USMF, Birch Company, Fourth Coffee East, Sparrow Retail.) |
| 2 | What's the business value / why does the buyer care? | ✅ | Finance teams burn analyst time on lookups/reconciliation across fragmented systems, and the industry quantifies it: cost per invoice $2.78 best-in-class vs $10.89 average, exception rates 9% vs 22% (Ardent Partners), CEI >85% = excellent collections, 1 in 3 CFOs distrust their own close data. A trustworthy agent must be *grounded* — hallucinated balances are worse than no answer. These benchmarks double as grading anchors. (`domain-workflows.md`) |
| 3 | Who are the stakeholders/personas in the workflow? | ✅ | Seven personas documented with recurring questions + done-criteria: AP specialist (3-way match, vendor statement recs), AR analyst (cash application, aging worklist), credit manager (limits, blocked-order release, review cadence), controller (subledger→GL tie-outs, close sign-off), treasury (daily cash position, bank portals), FP&A (variance commentary), procurement (supplier viability, Altman Z-score). (`domain-workflows.md` §1) |
| 4 | What's "considered done" per task family? | ✅ | Grounded per persona: AP = invoice matched/coded/approved/scheduled; AR = every receipt applied to specific invoices; credit = review documented on cadence with limit decision; controller = subledger ties to GL. Task-family mapping: ERP QA = exact figure + entity + as-of date + currency from tool calls; research = correct figure per filings within tolerance, period + source named; brief = required sections covered, figures accurate (hybrid 5-C's credit memo × equity-research structure per `domain-workflows.md` §2). |

## Tools & data

| # | Question | Status | Answer (evidence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | What is the primary ERP and its real API/tool surface? | ✅ | Dynamics 365 Finance. Domain model = three layers our mock must reproduce: posted subledger (`CustTrans`/`VendTrans`) → open remainder (`CustTransOpen`/`VendTransOpen`, carries the `DueDate` that drives aging) → settlement links (`CustSettlement`, partials + cash-discount-taken). Due dates from `PaymTerm`; cash discounts from `CashDisc` codes with next-discount chains (5D10%→10D5%→14D2%); collections = per-customer state machine (letter 1/2/…/Collection, grace days, fees); credit = `CreditMax` + check types + hold; **aging has two truths** (live bucketing vs batch snapshot) — divergence is eval content. Tool surface to mirror: Microsoft's dynamic ERP MCP server (discovery-first: `data_find_entity_type` → `data_get_entity_metadata` → `data_find_entities`/`_sql`, 13 form tools w/ 25-row pages, `api_find_actions`/`api_invoke_action`; static server retires 2026-10-01). OData entities verified: `CustomersV3`, `VendorsV2`, `PaymentTerms`. (`erp-domain.md`) |
| 10 | What competitor/adjacent tools should exist for chaos realism? | ✅ | Second system: **QuickBooks-Online-shaped subsidiary books** — richest documented MCP surface to imitate (official Intuit MCP, 144 tools; we mock a small subset), and small-entity books are exactly where subsidiary AR hides in real orgs. Plus: Excel shadow trackers (94% close in Excel — Ledge), email (68% of invoices manually keyed), bank-export file, EDGAR-shaped filings tool. Chaos mechanics chosen from research: divergent counterparty IDs across systems, Xero-style merged-contact history, stale shadow spreadsheet. Ramp's load-to-SQLite ETL pattern is the implementation model. (`erp-domain.md`, `domain-workflows.md`) |
| 11 | Which chaos patterns are realistic (and sourced), not invented? | ✅ | 12 sourced patterns in `domain-workflows.md` §3: invoices existing only in email; credit notes on vendor statements missing from ERP; approvals living in email threads; bank-cleared-but-not-posted timing gaps; decoupled remittance → unapplied cash (46% cite it); direct-to-GL JEs breaking subledger ties; GR/IR limbo (90-day/1-yr aged-item thresholds); near-duplicate invoices ("INV-5521" vs "5521-OPS"; ~1.5% of disbursements leak); spreadsheet version drift; Excel-only close trackers. Each seeded inconsistency will cite one. |
| 12 | How is public-company data served in-world (real web vs mocked provider)? | 🟡 | Mocked, EDGAR-shaped: full endpoint map researched (submissions / companyfacts / companyconcept / frames / full-text search) plus authentic friction candidates worth reproducing (10 req/s limit, mandatory User-Agent, 10-digit zero-padded CIKs, giant companyfacts payloads). Tool-shape references: stefanoamorelli/sec-edgar-mcp, financial-datasets/mcp-server (9 tools). Open: whether to reproduce the friction deliberately, and snapshot list (XOM FY2024, CAT 2023-2025, WMT FY2024, TSLA Q3-2025 + brief subjects). (`domain-workflows.md` §4) |
| 13 | What is the world epoch (fixed clock)? | ✅ | **2026-03-02T12:00:00Z**, set in `world/etl/load_core.py` and stamped into `meta.WORLD_NOW`. Confirmed by the extracts: every dataset.yaml erp_qa query and GT says "as of March 2, 2026"; journal dates run through 2025 into early 2026 (max due date 2026-04-16), so the epoch leaves both overdue and not-yet-due populations non-empty. |
| 17 | Are internal policy docs part of the world (and required reading for tasks)? | 🟡 | Lean yes: research produced concrete policies agents should have to *consult, not know* — 7-step dunning runbook with day thresholds (soft email d1-3 → call d14-17 → credit hold d30 → demand letter d45 → agency d60+), 2/10-net-30 discount-capture policy, credit-review cadence, ratio thresholds (current ratio 1.5–3.0; D/E <1 healthy, >3 over-levered; Z-score zones 2.99/1.81). House pattern: seed as anchor docs in an `agent_documents` table (salesforce-grok). Escalation lever: conflicting policy versions. (`domain-workflows.md` §2) |
