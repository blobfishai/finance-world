# FA-POL-03 — Production equipment capitalization

> SIMULATION ONLY · Contoso Entertainment System USA · Fixed Assets · effective 2026-01-01

Production equipment is available for use when commissioning has demonstrated that it can
operate in the manner intended by management. Capitalization stops on that date.

The equipment price, inbound freight, installation, and testing necessary to reach that
condition are directly attributable and capitalizable. Operator training is expensed when
incurred. Abnormal waste or rework after the asset is ready for use is also period expense,
even when the same supplier includes it on the installation invoice.

The fixed-asset review must cite the operative scope, final cost schedule, commissioning
evidence, purchase order, and receipt. Estimates and superseded scopes are not sufficient.
