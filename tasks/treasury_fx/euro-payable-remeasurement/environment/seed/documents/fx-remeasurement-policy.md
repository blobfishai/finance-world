# FX-POL-04 — Foreign-currency monetary items

> SIMULATION ONLY · Contoso Entertainment System USA · Treasury Accounting · effective 2026-01-01

Unsettled foreign-currency payables are monetary items and are remeasured at the closing
spot rate on the reporting date. The difference from the current functional-currency
carrying amount is recognized in period earnings as an exchange gain or loss.

Forward quotes, budget rates, and rates dated after the close are not closing spot rates.
They may inform cash planning but do not replace the accounting measurement. The close
workpaper must separately state the foreign-currency obligation, current carrying amount,
closing-rate amount, exchange difference, rate date, and contractual settlement date.
