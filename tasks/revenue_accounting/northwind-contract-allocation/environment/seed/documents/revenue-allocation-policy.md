# REV-POL-06 — Bundled arrangement allocation and cut-off

> SIMULATION ONLY · Contoso Entertainment System USA · Revenue Accounting · effective 2026-01-01

Transaction price is allocated to distinct performance obligations in proportion to their
standalone selling prices at contract inception. A bundled invoice amount is not evidence
that every obligation has been satisfied.

Appliance revenue is recognized when contractual acceptance transfers control. Support
revenue is deferred at inception and recognized over the stated service term. Acceptance
must be supported by the customer's immutable acceptance record; installation status or an
internal forecast is not a substitute.

For a USD 480,000 transaction with standalone prices of USD 300,000 for the appliance and
USD 200,000 for support, the relative allocation is 60 percent and 40 percent.
